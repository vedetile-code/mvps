Before you start

This is practice, not the official test.

TellMe Coach is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency. Scores are for rehearsal only and do not predict whether you will pass.

  • Tell-me answers are checked against the official GOV.UK points.
  • Show-me items have no official published model script. You will practise what you would do, sitting still. Confirm the controls in the car you use on test, with your instructor.
  • Do not practise show-me actions in a moving car except during a lesson or the test itself.
  • Always re-read the current official list before test day.
