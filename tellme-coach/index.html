Three steps

How a session works

  1. 01 · Hear it

    The examiner asks one official question.

    Questions are taken from the published GOV.UK list, licensed under the Open Government Licence. Nothing is invented to pad the bank.

  2. 02 · Say it

    You answer out loud, or type if the mic is unavailable.

    If a required official point is missing, you get one follow-up — the way an examiner presses on a thin answer — then a score against the rubric.

  3. 03 · Check it

    A debrief lists what was missing, with the official reference.

    It will not predict a pass. It will not call itself DVSA-approved. It will tell you to confirm the current list and practise show-me controls in the test car with your instructor.

What this is not

It is not the official test, not a DVSA product, and not a substitute for lessons. Show-me items have no official published model script — practice points describe a safe method only. Never practise show-me actions in a moving car except with an instructor, or on test.

Straight answers

Questions

Is this approved by DVSA?

No. TellMe Coach is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency or any government department.

Will this make me pass?

No tool can say that. A missed tell-me or show-me is usually a driving fault; a dangerous show-me action can fail the test. Lessons, practice and the official list still come first.

Do I need an API key?

Not for this public demo. Demo scoring checks your answer against the official points on your device. AI scoring is optional on a local copy of the app when an OPENAI_API_KEY is set in the environment. Keys must never be pasted into the public site or committed to the repository.

Who is this for?

Learner drivers sitting the car practical test in England, Scotland or Wales, and anyone retaking. It is sold to the individual candidate, not to a driving school.
