Free demos · secure checkout on each product page

Five consumer tools, each built for one buyer.

Each site works in the browser without an API key. The demos never take a card, and these pages do not send applications, listings, or exam results on your behalf. Paid plans open on a secure hosted checkout from each product page.

  1. 01 NookshiftHomeowners · renters · gardeners

    See a new look on a photo of your actual room or garden, then decide what to spend. Browser demo now; founding waitlist for AI restyles.

    →
  2. 02 ListReadyResellers

    Turn item photos and your notes into copy-ready listings for eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, and Vinted. Drafts only — it never posts or relists.

    →
  3. 03 TellMe CoachUK learner drivers

    Practise official UK driving-test “show me, tell me” safety questions out loud, with a debrief of the points you missed. Independent study aid, not affiliated with DVSA.

    →
  4. 04 QuireKDP self-publishers

    Build print-ready KDP interiors and cover wraps for journals, planners, and coloring books, measured to published trim, bleed, and gutter rules.

    →
  5. 05 ShiftReadyRegistered nurses

    A human-checked application pack for one registered-nurse job posting: resume, cover letter, interview questions, and a scored mock.

    →
