How it works

AI drafts. A person signs off.

01

You pay by card, PayPal, or Fiverr

Pick a pack and pay on a secure checkout page, or ask for a PayPal invoice. Work starts once payment clears.

02

You send a resume, the posting, and an intake

The intake collects achievements with numbers, licenses, and anything you do not want claimed. That last list is as important as the first.

03

Software extracts requirements and drafts from your facts

Requirements are mapped as supported, partial, or gap. The draft cannot add a certification, employer, or metric that is not in your inputs.

04

A human review checklist has to pass

Twenty to forty minutes of review. Automated fact flags are fixed first. The pack is not delivered until the reviewer signs off.

05

You receive files within 48 hours

Markdown and a print-ready layout for the resume and letter, plus questions or a scored mock depending on the pack. One revision is included if a fact was missed.

FAQ

Plain answers

Will this get me the job?

No promise of that is made. The work is a clearer, honest application for one posting, plus interview practice. Callbacks depend on the employer, the market, and your actual experience.

Why registered nurses, not every occupation?

Nursing applications turn on licenses, compact status, and certifications such as BLS, ACLS, or CCRN. Generic AI tools can invent those. The human fact-check is the product. The occupation also has substantial hiring volume: BLS reports about 180,800 RN openings a year on average for 2025–2035.

Is the public demo using a live model?

No. The site you are reading is a static demo. It can load a finished sample pack and can draft a generic pack from other pasted inputs in the browser. Live model drafts run only on a local copy of the tool when an OPENAI_API_KEY is set in the environment. Keys are never collected in this page.

What if the draft gets a fact wrong?

That is what the review checklist is for. If a missed fact is found after delivery, one revision is included.
