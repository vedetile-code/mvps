JOB TITLE: Progressive Care Unit Registered Nurse (Nights)
EMPLOYER: North Harbor Medical Center, Seattle, WA
POSTED: November 2026
SCHEDULE: 36 hours per week, night shift, 3x12-hour shifts, weekend and holiday rotation
UNIT: 28-bed progressive care / step-down unit. Typical staffing 3:1 or 4:1. Magnet-recognized hospital. Epic EHR.

ABOUT THE ROLE
North Harbor Medical Center is hiring night-shift RNs for our Progressive Care Unit. PCU nurses care for adult patients who need continuous telemetry, frequent assessment, and titratable infusions, including vasoactive drips.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Current RN license in Washington, or a compact (eNLC) multistate license
- Current BLS and ACLS (American Heart Association)
- At least 2 years of recent acute-care RN experience
- Telemetry experience
- Ability to work night shift, including weekends and holidays

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Progressive care, step-down, or intermediate-care experience
- Charge nurse experience
- Precepting or clinical-education experience
- Epic experience
- PCCN or CCRN certification

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Perform focused and ongoing assessments for PCU-level adult patients
- Monitor continuous telemetry and recognize and escalate rhythm changes
- Titrate vasoactive and other high-alert infusions per protocol
- Collaborate with the interdisciplinary team and give clear SBAR handoff
- Educate patients and families on the plan of care
- Participate in rapid response and unit quality work as assigned

COMPENSATION AND START
Night-shift differential, weekend differential, and relocation support may be available for qualified candidates. Start dates are rolling.
