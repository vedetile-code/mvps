Questions

Is the public demo a full AI redesign?

No. The live demo grades color, light and mood. It does not replace sofas or plants. That honesty is intentional: you can test the workflow on your photo today without uploading it. Full AI restyles need an API key in the local server, or a founding pass once checkout is live.

How do I pay if I live in, or the seller is in, Bermuda?

Paid software will go through Whop, Paddle or Dodo. Stripe is not offered in Bermuda, and Etsy shops cannot be opened from there. Human-labour marketplaces are not used for this product. Checkout links will be added only after those merchant accounts are approved. This page does not charge a card.

Should I trust a restyle when I buy paint or furniture?

No preview, demo or AI, is a substitute for samples, measurements, or a designer or contractor. Lighting and camera color can lie. Check every result against the real room before you spend.

What happens to my photo?

On this public demo, processing happens in your browser and the file is not uploaded. If you later run the local AI server, the photo is sent to the OpenAI API from your computer when you press Restyle. Do not upload photos you are not willing to send to that provider.

Who is Nookshift for?

An individual homeowner, renter or gardener. You are both the person who pays and the person who uses it. It is not sold as a tool for design firms, real-estate teams or hotels.
