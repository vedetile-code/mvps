The expensive mistake

Most rejected interiors fail the ruler, not the idea.

KDP trims 0.125 in off the top, bottom, and outside edge. The inside margin grows with page count, from 0.375 in to 0.875 in. Spine width is page count times a paper-specific factor. Get any of those wrong and the file comes back.

01

Gutter by page count

24–150 pages need 0.375 in inside. 151–300 need 0.5 in, and so on, up to 0.875 in. Quire sets this for you when the page count changes.

02

Bleed on the outside only

A 6 × 9 in book with bleed becomes a 6.125 × 9.25 in PDF. The extra width sits on the outside edge, not in the gutter. The preview draws that zone.

03

Cover wrap and spine math

The cover is one PDF: bleed, back, spine, front, bleed. Spine text is omitted at 79 pages or fewer, which is KDP’s rule, not a style choice.

Questions

Before you upload.

Is this affiliated with Amazon?

No. Quire is an independent layout tool. Amazon, Kindle, and KDP are trademarks of their owners. Always confirm measurements in KDP’s own help pages and calculators. Specs used here were read from KDP Help on 26 November 2026.

Do I have to disclose AI use on KDP?

Yes, if the book contains AI-generated text, images, or translations. KDP does not require disclosure of mere AI-assisted editing of work you wrote yourself. Demo interiors from this site use ruled layouts and procedural (non-model) art. If you add model-written prompts or model-made images, disclose them, even after heavy editing. See KDP content guidelines.

Can I publish as many books as I want?

Not each week. KDP limits new titles to two creations and two first submissions per format per week, resetting Sunday at 00:00 UTC. See Create a Book. This tool will not bypass that cap.

How do payments work?

By card on a secure hosted checkout page (Stripe). PayPal invoices are available on request. This site never sees your card.

Who is this for?

Individual self-publishers making journals, planners, logbooks, recipe books, or original coloring books for their own KDP accounts. It is not a service for publishing companies, and it will not auto-upload or bulk-submit titles.
