01 — How it works

Draft in one place. List by hand on each site.

eBay Magical Listing and Poshmark Smart List AI already write listings for free on those two sites. ListReady is for the rest of the job: one set of photos into five marketplace-specific drafts, including Vinted, plus a price range you verify yourself.

  1. 01

    Upload photos and notes

    Add 3–8 photos and what you already know: brand, size, measurements, flaws, cost, and where you plan to sell.

  2. 02

    Review the draft

    You get a title, description, item specifics, a variant per platform with character counts, a price range, and a sold-comps checklist.

  3. 03

    You paste. We never post.

    Copy the fields into the marketplace yourself. ListReady has no bots, no auto-posting, and no bulk relisting — on purpose.

  • eBay · 80-char title
  • Poshmark · 80-char title, 3 style tags
  • Mercari · 80 / 1,000 form caps
  • Depop · first line is the title, 5 hashtags
  • Vinted · 5–100 / 5–2,000

02 — What you get

Every field you would type, already written for the platform.

Each draft stays inside that marketplace’s typical limits, and the character count is on the field. Measurements and flaws are repeated on every version when you provided them. Uncertain details are flagged instead of invented.

Title

Keyword-led on eBay and Mercari; cleaner brand-colour-type wording on Vinted; first-line title on Depop.

Description

Facts from your photos and notes only: condition, measurements, materials, and disclosed flaws.

Item specifics

Brand, size, color, department, material, style, and condition — ready to enter as fields.

Price + comps checklist

A low / mid / high range, and steps to check sold listings yourself. We do not scrape marketplaces.

No bots Drafts only, by design

ListReady does not log into eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, or Vinted. It does not post, schedule, bump, favorite, or relist. Vinted’s terms prohibit using external software tools such as bots, scrapers, and crawlers on the site, and prohibit deleting and re-listing the same item multiple times or listing multiple items in bulk. Those features are not in this product and will not be added. You review every field and paste it yourself.

04 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Does this post to eBay, Poshmark, or Vinted for me?

No. It only writes drafts for you to copy. It will not post, relist, bump, or run a bot on any marketplace.

How is this different from eBay’s or Poshmark’s free AI?

Those tools are strong if you list only on that one site. ListReady is for sellers who cross-list, especially onto Vinted, Mercari, and Depop, and who want a price range with a checklist for checking sold comps by hand. If you only list on one site and its free built-in tool is faster for you, stick with that — ListReady earns its place when you cross-list.

Do you look up sold prices automatically?

No. Automatic sold-price lookup would mean scraping or logging into marketplaces, which we do not do. You get a starting range from the item details and a checklist to check sold listings yourself.

Is the live demo using AI on my photos?

The public GitHub Pages site is demo mode: three sample items with prepared drafts, and a notes-only fallback if you upload your own photos. Photo analysis needs an API key in a local server environment. No key is shipped with this site.

Who is this for?

Individual resellers and households selling their own secondhand items. It is not a tool for businesses to automate operations, and it is not a crosslisting bot.
